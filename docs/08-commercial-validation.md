# Rally commercial strategy — independent investment view

Date 2026-10-13. **Prices and examples are hypotheses**, not market quotations or evidence of paid adoption.

## What to sell

The desired outcome is **fewer unaccounted assets and fewer work orders stuck between execution, return and financial closure**, with faster discovery and traceable resolution. We do not sell Temporal, RFID readers, or an Odoo license as the core proposition.

### Starting SKU: Rally Custody Assurance

Possible commercial package:
- Per-tenant managed product subscription with asset register, custody, work-order linkage, exceptions, audit and operator interface.
- Implementation/setup fee for source mapping, equipment inventory, training and operational rule definition.
- Extra paid add-ons for Traccar fleet evidence, signed handover workflows, RFID edge integration, advanced SLAs and historical audit retention.
- Customer can continue using its chosen ERP; Odoo is a high-priority connector, not a mandatory dependence.
- Odoo itself, GPS SIM/data, devices, installation and additional licensing are separately scoped and priced.

## Explicit price experiments

| Revenue type | Experimental range |
| --- | --- |
| Rally basic recurring fee | US$99–299/company/month |
| Installation/onboarding | US$300–1,500 one time for tightly scoped cases |
| Custom integrations / hardware | Separately quoted after costs |
| White-label partner plan | Negotiated; no committed pricing |

A US$299 subscription costing a buyer 20 hours/month of labor at US$15/hour only matches US$300 in gross avoided labor. Onboarding and disruption make that marginal. To win, demonstrate additional prevented loss, reduced replacements, recovered invoicing, avoided downtime or measurable operations savings. **Do not count the face value of an invoice as newly recovered profit.**

The gross margin is highly sensitive to support hours, device mapping, custom Odoo modifications, hosting, sales cost and churn. Forecasts derived from hypothetical customer counts aren't validated unit economics.

## Product-led vs service-led distribution

- **Direct**: target a narrowly chosen asset-intensive sector and sell paid operational audits/pilots.
- **Channel**: Odoo partners, Traccar integrators, industrial equipment suppliers could refer or white-label Rally once support scope is standardized.
- **Professional services fallback**: if each project is bespoke, sell fixed-scope implementation services without pretending it is scalable self-service SaaS.
- Do not depend on uncertain current Thradex Telecom Odoo partnership status. Independently verify any official status before representing it in marketing.

## Discovery script

Ask the buyer to show last quarter's actual asset write-offs, overdue returns, field tasks without adequate completion evidence, lost equipment incidents, unresolved operational gaps, existing software and its workflows.

Measure baseline:
- Value at risk of missing assets (not total asset inventory).
- Hours investigating exceptions.
- Average time from field completion to invoice eligibility.
- Percentage of handovers with trustworthy acknowledgment.
- Percentage of known fault injections detected within a defined time.
- Actual total cost to deliver and support one tenant.

## Six-week decision proposal

1. **Weeks 1–2**: 20+ independent buyer interviews across Peru plus cross-market interviews; Odoo partners only a minority of evidence.
2. **Weeks 3–4**: identify five repeated, unresolved, expensive workflows and verify competitors don't already solve them affordably.
3. **Weeks 5–6**: three paid pilot commitments with narrow KPIs, implementation limits, access/privacy agreement and loss/savings model.
4. **Go/no-go**: authorize scale only when repeated paying demand and unit economics exist; otherwise narrow further or stop.

## Outcome claims to avoid

"All business transactions are guaranteed to succeed", "real-time physical proof from GPS", "loss-proof RFID", "fully certified for Odoo 19", "official Odoo partner" and "production ready" are **not substantiated** by MK0.
